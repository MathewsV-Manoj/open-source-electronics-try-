# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

OpenCircuit Academy is a learning community. We want participation to be welcoming and constructive for people with different levels of experience, educational backgrounds, identities and abilities.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, patient and considerate.
- Explain disagreements with evidence and focus on the work, not the person.
- Give beginner questions the same courtesy as advanced technical discussions.
- Accept constructive feedback and acknowledge mistakes.
- Credit original authors and respect licences.
- Keep discussions focused on learning and project improvement.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, insults, threats, doxxing, deliberate intimidation, sexualised attention, spam, and publishing another person's private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour in this project, contact the repository maintainer privately through the contact details on the GitHub profile. Include a link or description of the relevant interaction. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as possible.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close discussions, reject contributions, or block participation when necessary to protect the community. Decisions should be proportionate to the behaviour and communicated where practical.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
